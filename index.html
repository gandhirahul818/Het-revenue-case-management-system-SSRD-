🌐 Revenue Appeals Portal

The Special Secretary Revenue Department portal lets you check case status, next date of hearing, and order history. Since government portals require CAPTCHA and session login, this app opens the portal directly in your browser for secure access.

📋 Quick Links

🔗
Case Status Search Search by Revision Application number or party name on the portal.
🗺️
Survey No. Case Status Track status via Survey Number and village.
📄
Case Details / Orders View full case history, orders, and next hearing.

📖 How to Sync a Case Date

1
Open the Portal Tap any portal link above to open revenueappeals.gujarat.gov.in in Safari/browser.
2
Search Your Case Enter your Revision Application / case number in the Case Status page on the portal.
3
Note the Next Date Copy the next date of hearing shown on the portal for your case.
4
Update in App Return here, tap the ✏️ Edit button on your case, update the hearing date, and save — your reminder will auto-update.
